# Original versus futuristic map

`map_comparison_topdown.png` and `map_comparison_oblique.png` are rendered from identical cameras over
`blender/city_base.blend` (the untouched OpenStreetMap geometry) and `blender/city_future.blend` (Posta 2040).

What stays exactly the same:

- every building footprint (2,472 buildings, same polygons, same positions);
- all 439 roads, intersections and the rail line;
- the coastline, harbour, piers and terrain;
- the 829 trees, at their OSM positions.

What changes:

- building **height**: each building is scaled vertically by a factor driven by its distance to the Posta CBD, its
  footprint area and a seeded random, capped at 330 m, so the densest blocks become the tallest towers;
- 71 tall towers get a tapered glass crown and 63 get a spire with a beacon;
- materials: glass-and-steel curtain walls with lit windows, intelligent road surfaces with turquoise guidance lines;
- a harbour ferry and road vehicles.

Generated by `scripts/city_generation/build_future_city.py` and `scripts/rendering/map_comparison.py`.
